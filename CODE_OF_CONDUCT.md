# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a friendly, safe, and inclusive community.

Be respectful, assume good intent, give constructive technical feedback, and
avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or disclosure of private
information. Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict
participation when conduct harms the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to **akode.eu@outlook.com**. Reports will be
reviewed fairly and kept confidential to the extent practical.
